Privacy policy

ÇevirmenX sends only the text you choose to translate to an external translation service. The primary service is Google; MyMemory may be used as a fallback. These services receive the request and network information, including your IP address.

On your device

Translation history, favorites and translation memory are stored using browser local storage. Clear history and memory separately in the dashboard. Dictionary entries and preferences use your browser’s synchronized extension storage and may sync through your browser account.

Permissions

Page access lets the extension translate selected text and display the translation bubble. Context menus enable the right-click action. Storage saves preferences and dictionaries. Translation service permissions allow network requests to the named providers.

Website and support

The website stores language, theme and palette preferences locally. No advertising or analytics scripts are included. Hosting may process request logs. Buy Me a Coffee is an optional external service with its own privacy policy. Donations do not unlock features.

Your choices

Disable translation memory, erase local history or uninstall the extension at any time. Avoid sending sensitive text unless you accept the translation providers’ handling of that text.
